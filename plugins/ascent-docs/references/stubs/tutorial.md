## What you will build

Say in two sentences what the reader has at the end, and show it: the command output, the page, or the response they will see. A reader who cannot picture the result does not start.

## Before you start

List exactly what the reader needs, with the version this tutorial was tested with, and the one command that proves each is ready (for example `node --version`). Link to the setup guide rather than repeating it.

## Step 1: the smallest working thing

Get something running in the first step, even if it does almost nothing. Give the exact command or code, then what the reader should see. Every step ends with a visible result; a step with no result is where readers get lost.

## Step 2: add one idea

Each later step adds one idea to what already works. Explain only what the reader needs to understand this step, and link to the explanation for the rest.

## Check your work

Give the final command and its expected output, so the reader can tell success from a near miss. Name the two mistakes readers make most often and how to recognise them.
